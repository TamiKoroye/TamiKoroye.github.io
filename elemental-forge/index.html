ELEMENTAL FORGE

Four elements, four martial arts: Earth (Hung Gar), Fire (Northern Shaolin), Water (Tai Chi), Air (Baguazhang)

Controls

Move: left thumb on the left half (light push walks, full push runs, hold at the rim to sprint). Aim / camera: drag on the right half. LOCK frames the rival.

Elements (top left): switch instantly between Earth (Hung Gar), Fire (Northern Shaolin), Water (Tai Chi) and Air (Baguazhang). Each changes your stance, movement and every technique.

HAND tap: hand technique (stone punch, fire fist, water palm, air palm). Hold HAND for sustained bending: lift up to three rocks, a fire stream, draw water from a pool (then a water whip), or a wind push.

LEG tap: kicks (tap again for the second kick). Hold LEG and release for the charged move: seismic stomp, flying dragon kick, tidal sweep, cyclone.

Held rocks, flame orbs, water globes and vortices circle you; HAND or LEG launches them. SPECIAL: stone pillar (stand on it), flame orb (three = volley), water sphere (traps), vortex (three = big vortex).

GUARD (hold): earth wall, fire wall, water shield or air deflection. Raise it just before impact to send projectiles back and open a counter. DODGE: roll, flame dash, water slide or air dash. JUMP twice as Air; hold JUMP to glide.

Keyboard: WASD, mouse aim, left/right click = HAND/LEG, Q guard, E special, Space jump, Shift dodge, 1-4 elements, C sprint, Tab lock.
