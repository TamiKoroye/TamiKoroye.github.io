ELEMENTAL FORGE

Stage A build: skinned fighters, fluid movement, layered martial-arts combat, earthbending

Controls

Move: left thumb anywhere on the left half. Push lightly to walk, fully to run; hold the stick at its rim to sprint (when not locked on).

Aim / camera: drag on the right half. LOCK picks the rival nearest your view and keeps them framed.

STRIKE: tap for a jab, cross, spinning-kick combo. Hold briefly, then release, for a heavy palm strike (staggers, breaks guards). Hold until your fists glow for a charged axe-kick slam that knocks down everyone nearby.

GUARD (hold) raises a stone shield. Raise it just before a hit lands for a perfect guard: rocks fly back at the thrower, melee attackers stumble, and your next STRIKE becomes a spinning counter.

DODGE: roll in the stick direction. While locked on: sidestep left/right, hop back when pulling away. Dodge and jump cancel the end of an attack; buttons pressed slightly early are remembered.

BEND: tap to raise and throw a rock at the reticle; hold to keep it suspended, release to throw.

Keyboard: WASD move, C sprint, mouse aim (click to capture), Left click strike (hold for heavy/charged), Right click bend, Q guard, Space jump, Shift dodge, Tab lock, Esc pause.
