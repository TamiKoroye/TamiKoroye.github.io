# Air, phase one (from 20 Nov)

The user's brief: "Airbending combat implementation and Fire Daggers correction". Air is a Baguazhang fighter: circular
footwork, pivots, open-palm strikes, light quick attacks, the wind appearing with the movement. Twelve techniques,
opponent reactions by strength and direction, pale silver-white air (never smoke, never water), distinct air sounds, and
the existing phone controls only. References: the user's two airbending videos on the Drive ("15 Airbending Abilities"
and "18 More Airbending Abilities"), used for choreography and the look of the wind only.

## Control map (no new buttons)

| Input | Air |
|---|---|
| HAND | Air Blast, a compressed palm strike thrown as a ball of spiralling wind (15 m) |
| HAND, rival within 2.8 m | the close combination: palm strike, turning palm, gale kick (the kick staggers; it knocks down a rival already reeling) |
| HAND with the stick sideways | Air Swipe, an upright crescent of wind that drives the rival sideways (knockdown if they were mid-attack) |
| HAND with the stick pulled away | Suction, pulls the rival in to 1.6 m and leaves them stumbling (5 s cooldown) |
| LEG | Air Blades, two narrow blades from alternate hands (a stagger only when both land) |
| LEG, rival within 3 m | Air Bomb, a crouch then a radial burst: a rival within 2 m is thrown and knocked down (4 s cooldown) |
| LEG held | Air Funnel, a sustained cone of wind; the bender is planted, the rival braces and is driven back |
| LEG while running | the Running Kick every element has |
| GUARD | Air Shield: the arms circle and four bands of wind turn round the body; it deflects projectiles and turns blows aside |
| DODGE (with or without the stick) | Air Propulsion: a 0.5 s burst of wind carries the bender about 4.5 m along the floor, legs in the stance, a whirl round the legs. On the floor and in the air. No flips |
| JUMP | Air Spout: a spiralling column of wind from the floor lifts the bender into flight |
| JUMP held (in flight) | climb; let go and the bender sinks at 2.4 m/s. The stick steers and the body banks into the turn |
| HAND / LEG in flight | Air Blast / Air Blades aimed down at the rival |
| (automatic) | Air Cushion: an airbender falling fast is caught in the last 2.4 m, wind bursts across the floor and the landing is soft |
| SPECIAL | Air Vortex: the arms circle for 0.8 s while a cyclone winds up round the bender; it is sent at the rival, catches them, lifts and turns them, and throws them out |

## Opponent reactions

| Blow | Reaction |
|---|---|
| Air Blast, combo palms, single blade | light flinch (body, not head) |
| Gale kick, Swipe, both blades, Funnel after 1.2 s, Suction | stagger, in the direction of the blow |
| Air Bomb close, Swipe on an attacker, gale kick on a reeling rival | knockdown, thrown |
| Air Vortex | caught: lifted about 2.5 m and turned, then thrown down |
| any staggering wind blow on someone more than 0.8 m off the floor | blown out of the air, falling flat |

## Removed

- the two-second whirlwind glide on DODGE and the Corkscrew Evade / Cartwheel flips on every second dodge;
- the somersault on DODGE in the air (Air only);
- the Hurricane Kick on LEG in flight (now Air Blades);
- the Tornado's full-body spin (now the circling arms of the Air Vortex);
- the HAND whips and the LEG pressure wave / rising kick column.

## Sound

- A synthesised burst of compressed air (a high crack sweeping down into a whoosh) on the blast, swipe, blades and the
  propulsion.
- The wind loop held under the Funnel, flight and the Shield.
- A wind loop and a roar that build with the Vortex and peak while it carries someone.
