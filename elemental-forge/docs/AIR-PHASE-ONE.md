# Air, phase one (from 20 Nov)

The user's brief: "Airbending combat implementation and Fire Daggers correction". References: the user's two
airbending videos on the Drive, used for choreography and the look of the wind only.

## After review (20 Nov, r38)

"You were meant to ADD new skill sets to Air, not change it ... the old air bender was better, you just needed to add
more skills." r37 had replaced Air's dodge, HAND, LEG, jump, flight and Tornado. r38 puts every one of them back as it
was in r36 and adds the new skills only on inputs Air did not use.

## Air's moves as before (unchanged from r36)

HAND: Air Whip chain. HAND held: Twin Air Streams. LEG: kick chain; LEG charged: Air Compression. DODGE: the whirlwind
glide, every second dodge the Corkscrew Evade or the Cartwheel; in the air a somersault. JUMP: flight (JUMP held
climbs). In the air LEG: Hurricane Kick. GUARD: the whirlwind shield. SPECIAL: Tornado.

## Added

| Input | Added skill |
|---|---|
| GUARD + HAND | Air Blast: a compressed palm strike, a ball of spiralling wind (15 m, 8 damage, a flinch) |
| GUARD + LEG | Air Blades: two narrow blades from alternate hands (6 each; a stagger only when both land) |
| HAND with the stick pulled away from the rival | Suction: the rival hauled in to 1.6 m, stumbling (5 s cooldown) |
| LEG with the stick pulled away from the rival | Air Swipe: an upright crescent of wind that drives the rival sideways (9; a knockdown if they were mid-attack) |
| SPECIAL held 0.3 s, let go | Air Bomb: a crouch and a radial burst; a rival within 2 m is thrown and knocked down (12), up to 4.5 m staggered (8); 4 s cooldown. SPECIAL tapped is still the Tornado |

Not added for now (Air already has a move of that kind; available if wanted): the palm-palm-kick combination, the
sustained Air Funnel, the lateral wind-burst dodge, the spout take-off and leaning flight, the air cushion landing, the
Air Vortex, the four-band shield.

## Sound

- A short synthesised burst of compressed air on the Air Blast, Swipe and Blades.
- (r38, after review: "it makes a 'dumbp' sound when the character is hit") the synthesised boom added in the Earth
  sound round is no longer played when a rock, stomp or blow lands on a fighter; only the Earthbreaker's crash keeps it.
