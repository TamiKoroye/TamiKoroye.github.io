# Roadmap (from the owner, 17 Nov 2026)

The game is delivered as a link, opened in Chrome and installed from there as an app, so every update reaches players
without a new download: https://tamikoroye.github.io/elemental-forge-dev/ (dev build).

Planned layers, to be scoped and designed with the owner before any work starts:

- **Earth:** mud bending; striking from beneath the ground while fully submerged under the earth; later metal, lava
  and sand bending.
- **Water:** blood bending; drawing water out of the air; drowning the opponent in water orbs.
- **Fire:** lightning bending; devastating large-scale flames.
- **Air:** taking the air from an opponent and suffocating them, and further techniques.
- **Game structure:** layers of progression, villains ("bad guys"), a mini storyline, characters with back stories,
  skins and music.
